========================================================================
HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL MEDIA (PHOTOS, VIDEO & SONG)
========================================================================

Everything on this website is 100% functional out of the box with built-in
fallbacks, animations, synthesized sound effects, and romantic music!

Whenever you are ready to add your real personal files, simply place them in
this 'assets' folder with these exact names:

1. YOUR PERSONAL VIDEO MESSAGE:
   - Place your video file here and name it: `birthday_video.mp4`
   - Tip: Keep it under 50MB for smooth loading on her mobile phone.
   - Alternatively: Open `js/config.js` and paste your unlisted YouTube ID
     in `youtubeId: "YOUR_ID"`!

2. YOUR SPECIAL SONG (BACKGROUND MUSIC):
   - Place your favorite song here and name it: `song.mp3`
   - (Until you add it, the website automatically plays a soothing synthesized
     romantic starry lullaby arpeggio!)

3. REAL PHOTOS FOR MEMORY LANE (OPTIONAL):
   - Place your photos here named:
     `memory1.jpg` (GD College)
     `memory2.jpg` (Momos Date)
     `memory3.jpg` (Tulips)
     `memory4.jpg` (GT 650 Ride)
     `memory5.jpg` (Delhi)
   - (Currently, gorgeous glowing gradient polaroid cards with emojis are
     displayed so it already looks stunning!)

4. CUSTOMIZING TEXTS & QUESTIONS:
   - All questions, answers, banter hints, and handwritten love letters
     are cleanly stored in `js/config.js`. You can edit any sentence anytime!
